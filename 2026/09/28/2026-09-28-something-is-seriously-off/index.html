Something is seriously off and millions are noticing...

Created2026-09-28|Updated2026-09-28|英语乐园
|Post Views:

Summary

The speaker reflects on gradual changes in everyday surroundings, including insects, food, summer heat, and the feeling that familiar patterns have shifted. These notes preserve the speaker’s observations and interpretation in a punctuated English transcript.

Have you ever had the feeling that something is different, but you can’t quite figure out what it is? Not one big thing, but lots of little things. Maybe you’ve noticed more ticks, hotter nights, food that just doesn’t taste the same. Maybe you’re not seeing as many bugs on the windshield when you’re driving down the road, or fields that used to be full of fireflies are empty.

Most of these things haven’t changed overnight. They’ve happened slowly, over time, which has made it harder for us to notice them — until one day we look around and we notice that the world that we used to live in is almost non-existent anymore. The more people I talk to, the more I realize that I am not the only one that is feeling this. So many people seem to be looking around and noticing the same things. And I don’t think it’s nostalgia, and I don’t think that everything used to be perfect, but the life that used to feel normal to us is suddenly not feeling normal at all.

So today I want to share some of the biggest observations that I have made, and you can let me know in the comments if you have noticed these too, or what other things you have noticed.

The first one is just the amount of bugs. I remember when we used to go on vacation, we would drive from Tennessee down to the Gulf Shores, Alabama. It was like an 8 to 10 hour drive, and we would have to stop three to four times on our way down there to clean off our windshield, because it was covered — so just, like, full of bugs that you couldn’t even see out of the windshield. But as an adult, even driving around at night, you just don’t get the same amount of bugs on the windshield. And this is even in a rural area, where there should be tons of bugs.

The same thing with fireflies. When I was a kid, we used to catch fireflies all the time, all summer long, and me and all the neighborhood kids would go in the evenings and catch — we would take empty peanut butter jars that were clear, and we would fill up the peanut butter jars with hundreds of fireflies. And I was four, I wasn’t great at catching the fireflies, I would guess, but it was very easy to fill up an entire jar. And today, you just don’t see that amount of fireflies anymore.

At the same time, the tick population is exploding. I have seen videos of how bad ticks have gotten all across the US, and this is a huge issue, because the ticks that are around now are also carrying very high amounts of Lyme disease and other bacteria that is making people sick. When we were living in Virginia, we had some property — both me and my dog got Lyme disease at the same time and had to be treated, and all of that. We got Lyme disease and one other type of bacteria, I can’t remember what it was called, but the ticks, especially in like the northeastern part of the United States, are becoming very dangerous and abundant.

And then we also have this new alpha gal syndrome, which is making people actually allergic to meat protein and is causing people not to be able to eat meat. There’s a lot of theories around why the tick population is exploding so much. Some of the theories are it’s caused by man intentionally — it’s, it’s a man-made problem. But then also, you just have hotter weather, longer warm seasons, it’s not dipping below freezing for as long as it used to, and so the ticks just have the natural ability to be able to spread. So we’re kind of living in a world right now where the dangerous insects — ticks and even mosquitoes — those populations are increasing, while the beneficial bugs and insects, their populations are decreasing.

A lot of people talk about how food just doesn’t taste the same anymore. Tomatoes, strawberries, peaches — all of these foods kind of taste bland. It’s kind of like everything has just been muted, and we can’t quite taste the flavors that are supposed to be there. A lot of that has to do with our over-farming — the food is literally not as nutritious as it used to be. We’ve depleted our soil, and then the food companies are adding all of these chemicals and extra ingredients and manufactured things to our food. And so our food is, quite honestly, not the same that it used to be.

I’ve heard a lot of people say how summer nights feel different. At night, the temperature used to cool down, and you would wake up in the morning and things were crisp. But today, it just kind of stays hot all night long, in a lot of places where it used to get cool. And so you never really get that relief from the heat, especially in the summer. And when things are warm, it just, it just kind of stays hot and muggy all the time, which is causing people to need to run their air conditioning more, and just not enjoy the evenings nearly as much.

And even the sun during the day is feeling more intense. It’s like it is hotter, and you can’t stay out in it as long. I know when I was a kid, I could stay outside in the sun all day long, and even without sunscreen, I wouldn’t get burnt. And I mean, I’m pretty, pretty white, but I could be out all day and my skin was able to protect itself. You know, this was back in the 80s and 90s, and sunscreen wasn’t pushed as much as it is today, so it was pretty normal. But today, it’s a lot easier to get burnt by the sun. It seems like everybody has to be a little bit more careful, and a lot of that comes from awareness that if you do burn, you are at a higher risk for skin cancer, and skin cancer rates are increasing. But also, at the same time, that people are being more cautious and being more aware of putting on sunscreen or putting on extra clothing, and it almost feels like that’s a necessity these days — to wear rash guards and to stay out of the sun — because for some reason, our bodies just aren’t able to protect us in a way that it, it used to.

Being able to see the northern lights in very unusual places, all the way down to Florida — sometimes these northern lights are being able to be seen by people. When we were living in Virginia, we were able to see northern lights several times, which was extremely odd. You know, I — that was never even a possibility of something that I thought we would be able to see. It’s just something that was never heard of when we were growing up — being able to see the northern lights in Florida. You know, that was something that you had to travel to Canada or Alaska, or the northern states of the US, in Maine or Michigan or something like that.

Going out at night now, even in some pretty rural areas, you just can’t see the stars in the same way that you used to, and a lot of this has to do with light pollution. But, you know, there are so many stars in the sky. I have some pretty vivid memories — I used to lay out in my yard at night and just look up at the stars, and I would see shooting stars all the time, and we looked at constellations and all of that. And it just seems like it’s not that easy to do anymore — you have to go way out into a very rural area to be able to see more than, you know, a few stars at night. And when we would go to the beach in the summer, we could see the Milky Way very clearly. You just can’t do that anymore — you have to go out into a very, very rural area. I don’t even think people realize anymore — kids growing up today — realize quite how many stars there are up there, but, or even the Milky Way. A lot of people have never even seen the Milky Way.

One of the hardest things for me is the lack of silence in the world. Everything is so noisy, it’s hard to escape and get to a truly quiet, serene place. I think that’s one reason why I like to go hiking so much, is because you can get into the woods and into the forest, and the trees kind of mute all of the outside noise, and, uh, you’re kind of in this cocoon of just forest, and it’s not silent, but it is at least nature, and all of the outside noise of the world is, is blocked. But any city, any suburban area, any place where there’s anybody else around you, it is just very hard to have true silence anymore. This really can lead to a lot of overwhelm and not being able to ever disconnect from society. And, and noise is something that you kind of get used to, and you don’t necessarily hear it, you don’t hear a lot of the background noise, um, but it is something that your body is having to process and filter out.

There just used to be so many more spaces where people could go that were silent. There wasn’t so much development and traffic and noise of just everyday life. Construction — even now, I’m sitting here in a very rural area with the rice fields of Thailand, and birds singing all around me, but there’s also somebody weed eating the, um, rice fields, so I’ve got this noise of just a weed eater, which, you know, he’s working, whatever, but it’s still, even in this rural area, it’s not silent, because of the machines and the equipment that we have in our world, and just — it’s everything’s constantly going.

One of the strangest things, when I talk to people, that everyone seems to be identifying with, is how fast time seems to be moving and how quickly things are happening today. And, you know, yes, our perspective changes as we get older, because we have more time to compare it to, so the percentage of our life that a year is makes it seem as if it is shorter, because it’s a smaller percent of our life each year. And so our perception of time — it does make sense that things would speed up a bit. But also, just the world that we’re living in is so fast-paced, and everything is happening — you don’t really have enough time to just stop and be, and live in the moment, and be bored, and think about things, and just do things at a slower pace. And because we’re always so fast and rushing and just trying to get things done, it, it seems as if the way that we’re living our lives is making time go by faster.

And you can let me know what your opinion is on why time might seem like it’s going so fast, but that’s just been my conclusion of why everything goes so fast. You know, we only have one life to live, so I personally would like for life to slow down just a little bit, and that’s one of the things I’ve been working on — is trying to slow everything down, so that I have more life to enjoy and to experience. I don’t want it to rush by. I — I need it to slow down a little bit.

With all of these little tiny changes, it really does make something seem not quite right. I know that life is always changing and evolving, but the world that we used to live in really isn’t there anymore. And it’s not one big thing — I mean, the big things have happened and changed — but I think it’s the little tiny things that we need to pay attention to. The things that are most important are these little tiny signs that, that something is changing, and they’re the things that we really need to focus on. Most people are so wrapped up in all of the big things that we’re missing all the small things that are changing — fewer fireflies, fewer stars, less quiet, more screens, more noise, more stress.

And I’m not saying I need the world to go back to the way that it was, but I feel like all of these things that have changed are important. They were important then, and they’re important now. And I want to figure out why things have changed, because when something feels not quite right, when something feels wrong, maybe that’s the canary in the coal mine — the red flag that we need to pay attention to.
