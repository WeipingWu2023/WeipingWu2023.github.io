News Words: Illusion

Summary

This VOA vocabulary note introduces illusion through the Miami Paradox Museum. An illusion appears real even though it is not; an optical illusion makes the eyes perceive something differently from reality.

Content

以下为原笔记中的视频转录与学习内容，保留原素材的叙述语境。

A museum in Miami, Florida specializes in works of art that stretch the imagination. ILLUSION

The Miami Paradox Museum is one of several paradox museums across the world. More than 70 of its displays produce optical illusions worthy of a photograph The museum explains the math and science behind each illusion. An illusion is anything that is false or not real but that seems to be true or real. An optical illusion tricks your eyes to make you think you see something that is not present or that looks different in reality.
