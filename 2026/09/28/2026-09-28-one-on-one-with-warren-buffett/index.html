One-on-One with Warren Buffett

Created2026-09-28|Updated2026-09-28|英语乐园
|Post Views:

Summary

This historical interview with Warren Buffett covers the American economy, inequality, wages, tax credits, political donations, markets, philanthropy, and the American dream. The notes are organized into eight topics, each combining interview passages, vocabulary, and Chinese translation.

Watch on YouTube

Content

以下为原笔记中的视频转录与学习内容，保留原素材的叙述语境。

第一段：经济现状

断句后原文：

“We’re seeing some recent cracks in the US economy, right? You’ve got retail and auto sales down, you’ve got forecasts for first-quarter earnings looking lower than has been expected — to fall for the first time since 2012. Is this a blip, or is this the beginning of a bigger shift?”

“No, I don’t see any real weakness in the economy. Home building has been slow, and it continues slow, but the economy has been improving since the fall of 2009, and it continues to improve. So I just don’t see it. I’ve seen the February figures for our companies, and there’s parts that are a little stronger than others, but that’s been the case ever since the fall of 2009. We’ve been on an upward course, and people talk about double dips, and they talk about acceleration — in the end it just keeps moving ahead. And I think it’s quite a tribute actually to our policymakers, all the way back to the fall of 2008, that we’ve recovered like we have from what was an incredible panic.”

词汇与表达分析：

表达 含义
cracks in the economy “经济出现裂痕”——比喻经济开始显露隐患
a blip 一次短暂的、无关大局的小波动
double dip(s) “二次探底”，经济学术语，指经济复苏后再次下滑
a tribute to X 对X的一种肯定/证明（”这也证明了…的功劳”）
panic (经济语境) 特指”恐慌性金融危机”，如1929年大萧条前的”the Panic of 1929”

中文翻译：

“最近美国经济出现了一些裂痕，对吧？零售和汽车销售在下滑，第一季度盈利预期也被下调——这将是2012年以来盈利首次下滑。这只是一次小波动，还是更大转变的开始？”

“不，我没看到经济有什么真正的疲软。住房建设一直缓慢，现在依然缓慢，但经济从2009年秋天以来一直在改善，而且还在持续改善。所以我确实没看到（衰退迹象）。我看过我们旗下公司2月份的数据，有些板块确实比其他板块强一些，但这种情况从2009年秋天起就一直存在。我们一直处于上升通道，人们总在谈论’二次探底’，谈论’加速衰退’，但最终经济总是在往前走。我认为，从2008年秋天算起，这其实要归功于我们的政策制定者——我们能从那场惊人的恐慌中恢复过来，实属不易。”

第二段：耶伦讲话与经济风险

断句：

“Fed Chair Janet Yellen said on Friday: if underlying conditions had truly returned to normal, the economy should be booming. Where’s the risk?”

“Well, I don’t think there’s a particular risk in the economy. Over time we will have recessions from time to time, and we had a real doozy in the fall of 2008 and early 2009, and that has had more consequences than other recessions have had post–World War II. But the American economy is a wonderful machine — it really works. It’s worked since 1776, and it’ll continue to work. But we will continue to have ups and downs, because for some Americans — they listen to that and they say, ‘It’s not working for me,’ and they see stagnant wage growth and they feel like it’s getting increasingly hard to get by year by year. So who’s winning, Wall Street or Main Street?”

“The extreme rich are clearly winning.”

词汇分析：

表达 含义
a real doozy 口语，”一个厉害的家伙/事件”，这里指”一场非常严重的危机”
post–World War II 二战后
a wonderful machine 比喻美国经济体系是一台运转良好的机器
stagnant wage growth 工资增长停滞
get by 勉强维持生计
Wall Street vs. Main Street 华尔街(金融资本)对普通民众(实体经济/平民)的经典对照表达

翻译：

“美联储主席珍妮特·耶伦周五说：如果经济基本面真的恢复正常，经济现在应该是一片繁荣。那风险在哪？”

“我不认为经济存在特别的风险。从长期看，我们总会时不时经历衰退，2008年秋天到2009年初我们经历了一场真正严重的危机，它带来的后果比二战后其他任何一次衰退都更大。但美国经济是一台了不起的机器——它确实运转良好，从1776年至今一直如此，未来也会继续运转下去。但我们仍会有起有落，因为对一些美国人来说，他们听到这些话会说’这对我不管用’，他们看到工资增长停滞不前，感觉一年比一年更难维持生计。那么到底是华尔街赢了，还是普通老百姓赢了？”

“显然是极端富有的那群人赢了。”

第三段：贫富差距（Forbes 400）

断句：

“If you look at the Forbes 400, they had an aggregate net worth of $92 billion in 1982. It’s $2.3 trillion today — 25 times as much. So the very rich have done exceptionally well in this economy. Other people have done okay on balance, but it’s certainly the super-rich — it’s really been concentrated. You can just look at that figure: from $92 billion to $2.3 trillion.”

词汇分析：

表达 含义
aggregate net worth 总净资产
have done exceptionally well 表现极为出色（这里指财富暴增）
on balance 总体而言，权衡利弊后
concentrated (财富语境) 财富”集中”于少数人手中

翻译：

“看看《福布斯》400富豪榜，1982年他们的总净资产是920亿美元，如今是2.3万亿美元——是原来的25倍。所以在这个经济体系里，最富的人表现极为出色。其他人总体过得还可以，但财富确实是向超级富豪集中——从920亿到2.3万亿，这个数字本身就说明问题。”

第四段：最低工资 vs. 收入所得税抵免

断句：

“But when it comes to wages, we’ve seen Walmart, for example, and some other companies follow in its footsteps, raising what they will pay their entry-level workers up to $10 by 2016.”

“Last year when I asked you, Warren, if the federal minimum wage should be raised from $7.25 an hour, you said, ‘That is the toughest question you could ask me, because I’ve been thinking about it for 50 years and I don’t know the answer.’ And your reason for that is because you don’t know if more people will be better off, because you do lose some employment.”

“I know if you increase it to $20, I’d like to see everybody make $20 an hour at a minimum, but I know if you increase it to $20 an hour, you’d have millions of people unemployed. I don’t know how to calibrate it precisely. I personally believe that the Earned Income Tax Credit is a much better way of handling the problem of people who are really not paid enough to live decently, and the Earned Income Tax Credit accounted for $56 or $57 billion in 2013. I would expand that a lot, and I would do it smarter too, but I think that’s more effective in helping the people who are really at the lower end than actually a change in the minimum wage.”

词汇分析：

表达 含义
follow in one’s footsteps 跟随某人的脚步/效仿
entry-level workers 入门级/基层员工
calibrate 精确校准、调整（这里指精确评估最低工资该定多高）
live decently 体面地生活
the Earned Income Tax Credit (EITC) “劳动所得税抵免”，美国一项针对低收入劳动者的税收补贴政策
the lower end 收入底层人群

翻译：

“但说到工资，我们看到沃尔玛这样的公司，还有一些跟风的企业，把入门级员工的时薪提高到2016年前达到10美元。”

“去年我问你，联邦最低工资是否应该从7.25美元上调，你说：’这是你能问我的最难的问题，因为我已经想了50年，还是没有答案。’你说这是因为你不确定提高最低工资是否真能让更多人受益，因为这样做确实会导致一部分人失业。”

“我知道如果把它提高到20美元——我确实希望每个人的时薪至少能到20美元——但我也知道如果真涨到20美元，会有几百万人失业。我不知道该怎么精确拿捏这个度。我个人认为，’劳动所得税抵免’（EITC）是解决’工作但收入不足以体面生活’这个问题的更好方式，这项政策在2013年的规模是560或570亿美元。我愿意大幅扩大它的规模，而且做得更聪明一些，但我认为，比起直接调整最低工资，这对真正处于底层的人帮助更大。”

第五段：为什么该关心收入不平等

断句：

“Why should people care about income inequality?”

“Well, I think that we live in a country that has $54,000 of GDP per capita, and it’s the richest country in the world. And America, in many ways — real GDP is six times what it was per capita when I was born — but all kinds of people are left behind, and there are some structural reasons for that. And I think that a rich family takes care of the people that aren’t born with a particular wiring that enables them to prosper mightily in a market economy, and there’s lots of people that — they fight in Afghanistan, they do all — you know, through no fault of their own, their skills are not skills that the market values highly. And I think that a rich society, a very rich society, can figure out ways to have those people do better than they are.”

词汇分析：

表达 含义
GDP per capita 人均国内生产总值
be left behind 被落下、被时代抛下
structural reasons 结构性原因
(particular) wiring 比喻用法，”天生的头脑构造/禀赋”，指与生俱来适应市场经济的能力
prosper mightily 极为兴旺发达
through no fault of one’s own 并非某人自身的过错
market values highly 市场高度看重（某种技能）

翻译：

“为什么人们应该关心收入不平等？”

“我认为，我们生活在一个人均GDP高达5.4万美元的国家，是世界上最富有的国家。而且从很多方面看，美国现在的人均实际GDP是我出生时的六倍——但各种各样的人依然被落在了后面，这背后是有一些结构性原因的。我认为，一个富裕的家庭会照顾那些天生没有具备在市场经济中大展身手的’先天禀赋’的成员；同样地，社会上也有很多人——他们在阿富汗打仗，他们做了很多事——他们的技能并非市场所看重的技能，这不是他们自己的错。我认为，一个富裕的社会，一个非常富裕的社会，是可以想办法让这些人过得更好的。”

第六段：政治献金与民主

断句：

“With Citizens United and other decisions that enable the rich to contribute really unlimited amounts, that actually tilts the balance even more toward the ultra-rich. So the ballot box is the ultimate answer for changes in policy, but the unlimited giving to parties and to candidates really pushes us more toward a plutocracy. You know, they say it’s free speech, but somebody can speak 20 or 30 million times, and my cleaning lady can’t speak at all.”

词汇分析：

表达 含义
Citizens United 指2010年美国最高法院著名判例”公民联盟案”，裁定放开企业/团体的政治献金限制
tilt the balance 使天平倾斜，打破平衡
the ballot box 投票箱，代指”选举制度/民主投票”
plutocracy 富豪统治、财阀政治
free speech 言论自由（此处巴菲特讽刺地用它来指代”花钱等于说话”）

翻译：

“随着’公民联盟案’等一系列判决允许富人几乎无限制地捐款，这实际上让天平更加向超级富豪倾斜了。投票箱本应是政策改变的最终途径，但政党和候选人可以无限制地接受捐款，这实际上正把我们推向一种’财阀统治’。他们说这是言论自由，可问题是，有人能’说话’两三千万次，而我家的清洁工阿姨却一句话都说不了。”

第七段：股市与希腊退欧

断句：

“You said this year: periodically financial markets will become divorced from reality — you can count on that. Are US markets that way right now?”

“Not yet, but they’ve moved a long way up, and they could get — I mean, we could have overpriced stock markets. We’ve had them at various times in the last 150-plus years, and we’ll have them again. It’s always easier to identify them in retrospect. When the market went on the S&P from 666 or so to 2100, I mean, that is a big move, but stocks were very cheap then. They might be a little on the high side now, but they’ve not gone into bubble territory.”

“What about a tech bubble? Are we living 1999 all over again? The Nasdaq’s right near that 5,000 level.”

“It’s a different composition to some extent now. Apple deserves a lot — they were eyeballs then, and they’re profits now in many cases. I do not regard us as being in bubble territory, but I don’t find cheap stocks to buy either.”

“Do you think we will see a Greek exit from the Eurozone?”

“I don’t know whether we’ll see it. I do know that the euro has had certain structural weaknesses since it was formed — that doesn’t mean it isn’t a good idea, it doesn’t mean that they can’t be corrected. But putting together a group of countries with significantly different fiscal policies, labor laws, cultures, and then trying to tie them all to a single currency can present strains as you go down the road, and as we’re seeing — they either have to learn to harmonize some other important economic characteristics of the countries more, or they can’t all stay on a common currency.”

词汇分析：

表达 含义
divorced from reality “脱离现实”，指市场价格脱离基本面
overpriced / underpriced 估值过高/过低
in retrospect 事后回顾来看
bubble territory “泡沫区域”，指资产价格已进入泡沫状态
eyeballs (互联网泡沫术语) 指1999年互联网公司靠”流量/点击量”而非实际盈利估值
Grexit（隐含） “希腊退出欧元区”的俗称（文中未直接出现该词，但语境即此）
harmonize （政策、法律等）协调一致
fiscal policies 财政政策

翻译：

“你今年说过：金融市场会周期性地脱离现实，这是必然的。美国市场现在是这种状态吗？”

“还没有，但已经涨了很多，也可能——我是说，我们确实可能出现估值过高的股市。过去150多年里，我们经历过好几次这种情况，未来还会再出现。这种泡沫总是事后看更容易辨认。标普500从666点左右涨到2100点，这确实是很大的涨幅，但那时候股票非常便宜。现在可能有点偏高了，但还没有进入泡沫区域。”

“那科技股呢？我们是不是又回到1999年了？纳斯达克现在接近5000点。”

“现在的构成成分已经有所不同了。苹果这样的公司值这个价——1999年那批公司靠的是’流量’，现在很多公司靠的是实实在在的利润。我不认为我们处于泡沫之中，但我也确实找不到便宜的股票可买。”

“你认为希腊会退出欧元区吗？”

“我不知道会不会发生。我知道欧元自诞生起就存在一些结构性的弱点——这不代表欧元本身不是个好主意，也不代表这些问题无法纠正。但把一群财政政策、劳动法律、文化都相差巨大的国家捆在一起，用同一种货币，长期下来必然会产生压力，而我们现在正看到这一点——这些国家要么在其他重要的经济特征上进一步协调统一，要么就无法继续共用一种货币。”

第八段：慈善、贫困与美国梦

断句：

“What is the best thing that can be done to reduce poverty on a global scale?”

“At the Gates Foundation, we think that health is probably the number one thing. I mean, if you aren’t healthy, and you don’t have access to vaccines and other aids to better health, you really don’t have anything going for you.”

“You’ve pledged almost all of your wealth to philanthropy — 99% plus — and a lot of other billionaires have joined in that with you. What global issues can be solved through philanthropy, not just relying on the marketplace or capitalism?”

“We won’t solve anything in the ultimate — I mean, we’re never going to get 7 billion people all as educated as we’d like, or all as healthy as we’d like — but things can be done in a major way in terms of health, in terms of family planning, in terms of education. And you try to figure out — if you really believe that every human life is of equal value, that means a different thing in some impoverished nation than it means in the United States, and you simply try to improve the lot of humans wherever they may be, just like your own children.”

“Is the American Dream still alive and well?”

“The American Dream is alive and well, but it isn’t for a good many people. It’s been alive and well for a lot of people, but there are people that — the American Dream is based in part on a market system and the rule of law and equality of opportunity, but a market system is going to leave a lot of people behind, and that’s where government comes in — that’s why we put in Social Security and some things of that sort. The American Dream, in the sense that anybody can move up into some top economic class, or become president of the United States — that is a dream, and social mobility has always been relatively good in this country compared to the rest of the world. But there are limitations to the American Dream: if someone is born with subnormal intelligence, they can have some dreams, but they’re not going to move into the top economic class, in all probability, unless they happen to be the child of some super-rich person.”

词汇分析：

表达 含义
have access to 能够获取/使用
have something going for you “有奔头/有依靠”，口语表达
pledge one’s wealth to philanthropy 承诺将财富捐给慈善事业
in the ultimate 从终极意义上说
the lot of humans “人类的命运/处境”（lot作名词表示”境遇”）
alive and well （事物/理念）依然存在且有活力
rule of law 法治
equality of opportunity 机会平等
Social Security 美国”社会保障”制度（养老/福利体系）
social mobility 社会流动性
subnormal intelligence 低于正常水平的智力

翻译：

“从全球范围看，减少贫困最好的办法是什么？”

“在盖茨基金会，我们认为健康可能是第一位的。如果一个人身体不健康，又无法获得疫苗和其他改善健康的手段，那他基本上就没什么指望了。”

“你已经把几乎全部财富——99%以上——都承诺捐给了慈善事业，很多其他亿万富翁也跟你一起这样做了。有哪些全球性问题是可以靠慈善解决的，而不只是依赖市场或资本主义？”

“从终极意义上说，我们解决不了任何问题——我是说，我们永远无法让70亿人都接受到我们理想中的教育，或都拥有理想中的健康水平——但在健康、计划生育、教育这些方面，我们是可以做出重大改变的。你要想明白一件事：如果你真心相信每一个人的生命价值都是平等的，那么这句话在一个贫困国家和在美国的含义是不一样的，你只是单纯地想改善各地人们的处境，就像对待自己的孩子一样。”

“美国梦还存在吗，还有活力吗？”

“美国梦依然存在、依然有活力，但对很多人来说并非如此。它对很多人来说一直有活力，但也有一些人——美国梦部分建立在市场体系、法治和机会平等之上，但市场体系必然会让很多人掉队，这时候就该政府出手了——这也是为什么我们建立了社会保障制度之类的东西。’任何人都能跻身顶层经济阶层，甚至当上美国总统’这种意义上的美国梦，确实是一个梦想，而美国的社会流动性，相比世界其他地方，一直算是相对不错的。但美国梦也是有局限的：如果一个人生来智力低于正常水平，他可以怀抱一些梦想，但他大概率无法跻身顶层经济阶层——除非他碰巧是某个超级富豪的孩子。”
